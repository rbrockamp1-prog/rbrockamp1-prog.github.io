Design System — FINRA UI

Moving a design system from Adobe XD to Figma, and the team with it.

Overview

Product design and design systems work that included FINRA’s transition from Adobe XD to Figma, a massive effort across the organization.

Ricky took a leading role in the collaboration, assembling a team of volunteers who rebuilt every component using modern techniques and deep Figma knowledge.

Client
FINRA
Role
Product designer, design lead
Focus
  • Design systems
  • Figma migration
  • Theming
  • Accessibility

What I did

  • Led the migration

    Coordinated the move of the full component library from Adobe XD to Figma.

  • Built a volunteer team

    Assembled designers who rebuilt all components with modern Figma techniques.

  • Upskilled 20+ designers

    Created the curriculum and training program that brought the design team up to speed.

  • Extended through theming

    Added theming that supports a partner brand and accessibility features like dark mode.

  • Set up governance

    Established design governance so the system stays consistent as it grows.
